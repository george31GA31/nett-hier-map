WHAT TO PLOT

Stickers

This page sets out which sticker sightings belong on the map.

Accepted

Which stickers count?

Only the yellow oval sticker in this same format is accepted: either the German original reading “Nett hier. Aber waren Sie schon mal in Baden-Württemberg?” or the English translation “Not bad. But have you ever been to Baden-Württemberg?”.

Other colours, shapes, redesigns, parodies or unrelated Baden-Württemberg stickers should not be plotted.

A quick note

This site is just a bit of fun for documenting stickers that are already out in the world. We do not condone or endorse vandalism, trespassing, fly-posting, or defacing public or private property. Please only photograph stickers safely and lawfully, and do not place stickers anywhere you do not have permission to put them.
