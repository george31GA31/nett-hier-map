Plot accurately

Place the pin as close to the sticker as possible

Please do not just click the city where you found it. Zoom in and place the pin at the exact spot where you saw the sticker. If you are standing there, use “Plot my location” for the most accurate result.

A quick note

This site is just a bit of fun for documenting stickers that are already out in the world. We do not condone or endorse vandalism, trespassing, fly-posting, or defacing public or private property. Please only photograph stickers safely and lawfully, and do not place stickers anywhere you do not have permission to put them.

This is an independent, non-commercial fan project. I have never made, do not make, and will never make any money from this website. To buy genuine stickers or merchandise, visit the official THE LÄND shop.
